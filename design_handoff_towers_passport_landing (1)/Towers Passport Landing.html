One partner, one program, the whole life of the tower.

FORM

BUILT

08 · Straight answers

A few practical details.

01Do you replace our selling agents?

No. We sharpen your agents' edge — campaign-ready assets, share links and lead briefs. Negotiation and buyer relationships stay with your agents.

02Do our surveyors and consultants stay?

Yes. Your licensed surveyors stay. Architects, engineers and builders all work from the same visual record.

03What does our team need to install?

Nothing new to learn. Share links and website embeds work on any device — no apps, no downloads — alongside Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud and your project tools.

04Who owns the data?

You do. Exports are in open 3D formats (SPZ, PLY, GLB) aligned with the glTF splat standard.

05How often do you scan during construction?

At the cadence your project needs — fortnightly, monthly or by milestone: pre-formwork, pre-plumbing, pre-gyprock, pre-fit-off.

06Who is on our site?

A dedicated capture technician on a fixed cadence, with a licensed building professional's eye over every dataset. Inductions, safety systems and insurances in place.
